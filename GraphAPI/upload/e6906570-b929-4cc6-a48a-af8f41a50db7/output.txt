Title: Employment Agreement​
 Effective Date: 1 March 2024 
This Employment Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into between Orion Tech Solutions 
Pvt. Ltd., a company incorporated under the laws of India, having its registered office at 
Bengaluru, Karnataka (“Employer”), and Rahul Sharma, residing at Pune, Maharashtra 
(“Employee”). 
1. Position and Duties 
The Employee is appointed as a Software Engineer and shall report to the Engineering 
Manager. The Employee agrees to perform duties assigned by the Employer in accordance 
with company policies. 
2. Term of Employment 
The employment shall commence on 1 March 2024 and shall continue unless terminated in 
accordance with this Agreement. 
3. Compensation 
The Employee shall receive an annual CTC of INR 12,00,000, payable in monthly 
installments. Performance-based bonuses may be granted at the Employer’s discretion. 
4. Confidentiality 
The Employee shall not disclose any confidential or proprietary information of the 
Employer during or after the term of employment. 
5. Termination 
Either party may terminate this Agreement by providing 30 days’ written notice. The 
Employer may terminate immediately in cases of misconduct or breach of confidentiality. 
6. Governing Law 
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. 
7. Jurisdiction 
The courts of Bengaluru, Karnataka shall have exclusive jurisdiction over disputes arising 
from this Agreement. 
Signed on: 1 March 2024​
 Employer: Orion Tech Solutions Pvt. Ltd.​
 Employee: Rahul Sharma
